<h1>Glúnta Demographic Map</h1>

<p>
  An interactive demographic and mission research platform for the Republic of Ireland.
</p>

<p>
  <strong>Live Site:</strong>
  <a href="https://gluntaireland.github.io/glunta-demographic-map/">https://gluntaireland.github.io/glunta-demographic-map/</a>
</p>

<h2>Overview</h2>

<p>
  The Glúnta Demographic Map combines Census 2022 demographic data, public administrative boundaries,
  church-location datasets, and mission-focused analysis to help churches, church planters, researchers,
  and denominational leaders better understand communities across Ireland.
</p>

<p>
  The project exists to support informed prayer, research, church planting, ministry planning, and strategic
  thinking by making demographic and geographic information accessible in a practical format.
</p>

<h2>Features</h2>

<h3>Demographic Mapping</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Counties</li>
  <li>Local Electoral Areas (LEAs)</li>
  <li>Urban Areas and Towns</li>
  <li>Small Areas within major cities</li>
</ul>

<h3>City Detail Mode</h3>

<p>Detailed Small Area exploration is available for:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Dublin City and Suburbs</li>
  <li>Cork City and Suburbs</li>
  <li>Galway City and Suburbs</li>
  <li>Limerick City and Suburbs</li>
  <li>Waterford City and Suburbs</li>
</ul>

<h3>Town Mission Profiles</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Population overview</li>
  <li>Religious affiliation</li>
  <li>Migration and birthplace</li>
  <li>Language usage</li>
  <li>Family and household structure</li>
  <li>Housing characteristics</li>
  <li>Education and employment</li>
  <li>Church presence</li>
  <li>Mission observations</li>
  <li>Printable PDF reports</li>
</ul>

<h3>LEA Mission Profiles</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Demographic summaries</li>
  <li>Church presence</li>
  <li>Mission observations</li>
  <li>Printable reports</li>
</ul>

<h3>Church Presence and Unreached Town Layers</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Visualise church locations throughout the Republic of Ireland.</li>
  <li>Identify towns and settlements with limited or no known evangelical church presence.</li>
</ul>
