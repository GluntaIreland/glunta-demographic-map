About this map

The Glúnta County Demographic Map is an interactive visualisation of Census 2022 demographic data for Ireland’s administrative county and local authority areas.

It is designed to help churches, church leaders, mission agencies, researchers and interested members of the public better understand the demographic shape of communities across Ireland.

What this map does not show

This map does not measure spiritual need, gospel openness, church health or ministry priority by itself. It provides demographic context. The data should be interpreted carefully, locally and alongside other forms of knowledge, including church presence, community relationships, local history and pastoral judgement.

Data sources

Demographic data comes from the Central Statistics Office Census 2022 Small Area Population Statistics. Boundary data comes from Tailte Éireann / data.gov.ie administrative area boundaries.

Geography

This version uses administrative counties and local authorities. These are not always the same as traditional counties. For example, Dublin is divided into Dublin City, Fingal, South Dublin and Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown. Cork and Galway are also divided into city and county local authority areas.

Percentages

Most percentages are calculated using the relevant Census table total for each area. Some language percentages are calculated using total population as the denominator. Principal economic status percentages use the population aged 15 years and over.

Church overlay

The church-location overlay is included for visual context only. It is not intended to replace the full Glúnta Church Map and does not include denomination, tradition, church-density or opportunity analysis.

Limitations

Census categories are broad. They should not be overinterpreted. Religious identity, ethnicity, language, citizenship and family structure are complex realities that cannot be fully represented in a map. This tool is intended to prompt better questions, not provide final conclusions.
