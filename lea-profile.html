08 · Glúnta Field Guide

How to understand this LEA

Use the area-wide picture to decide where to listen more closely. Visit different communities and connect what you learn across the region.

1. Check the geography

Confirm the Census year and statistical boundary. Identify the neighbourhoods, settlements and rural areas it includes. Note important places just outside it.

2. Compare local experiences

Visit several parts of the LEA. Ask whether age, housing and cultural patterns differ between them. An area-wide percentage should not be assigned to every neighbourhood.

3. Follow everyday journeys

Ask where people travel for school, work, care, shopping and friendship. Check transport, journey times and the practical accessibility of church gatherings.

4. Meet existing churches

Verify the listings and listen to leaders and members. Ask about their relationships, convictions, history and capacity, and where collaboration could be fruitful.

5. Record community assets

Find trusted people, libraries, schools, clubs, community organisations and informal meeting places. Record what residents value, with a source and date.

6. Choose a shared next step

Discuss your observations with local partners. Agree one practical way to listen, serve or develop discipleship. Return to ask whether it helped and what needs to change.

A listening and prayer route

  1. Choose two or three contrasting places within the LEA. Plan realistic travel between them and respect private property.
  2. Visit at different times. Notice gathering places and access, without assuming what happens inside a building.
  3. When conversation is welcome, ask what people value, what has changed and who else you should hear.
  4. Keep direct observation, residents’ accounts and your interpretation distinct. Ask permission before recording or quoting anyone.
  5. Finish with three supported observations, two unanswered questions and one agreed next step.

Your local research notes

Record: place or topic · source or person’s role · date · evidence type · uncertainty · next action. Name the particular community rather than attributing every observation to the whole LEA.

09 · Evidence & limits

Read the evidence carefully

  • Demographics: CSO Census 2022 Small Area Population Statistics , published Local Electoral Areas table (166 areas). Download the table used here . Census date: 3 April 2022. The report selects the published LEA row by its code and verifies its GUID against the mapped boundary.
  • Geography: Census 2022 statistical LEA boundaries supplied with Glúnta . CSO explains that some statistical LEAs differ from statutory electoral boundaries. The report flags the supplied boundary’s adjustment indicator. The source Irish Transverse Mercator coordinates are converted for the map and church-point checks using Proj4js. These are 2022 geographies, not a claim about current electoral arrangements.
  • Church records: Glúnta’s supplied church-points file . Its update date and coverage have not been established here. Listings are not a complete census of congregations or all faith communities. Coordinates and current activity require local verification.
  • Basemap: the interactive map uses OpenStreetMap contributors . The printable diagram shows a simplified boundary and listed church points, without basemap tiles.
  • Presentation inspiration: Brad Brisco’s sample report and Field Guide . The figures here come from the Irish sources above.
Published statistic
A reported count with a stated geography, year and denominator.
Organisation record
A supplied listing whose coverage and currency need local verification.
Local account
A person’s experience, recorded with permission and context; not a population estimate.
Interpretation / question
A suggested implication or discussion prompt. The mission cards use this category.
Unavailable
Missing, withheld or unverified information. It must not be read as zero.

Each chart uses its own published denominator. “Not stated” stays visible where supplied. Displayed percentages may total slightly above or below 100% because of rounding. LEA-wide figures can conceal local variation.

Income, deprivation scores and verified local narratives are not included in this edition. Do not infer them from housing tenure, birthplace, religion or education.
