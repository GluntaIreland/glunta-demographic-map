Begin with local listening

This profile is intended to support prayer, partnership, and research. It should not be read as a mechanical church planting recommendation.

Read the data alongside people

Demographic data can show patterns, but it cannot tell the full story of relationships, history, local trust, existing witness, or spiritual openness.

Data note

Sources and method

This profile draws from Census 2022 Local Electoral Area boundaries, Small Area boundaries, processed Small Area demographic tables, and the Glúnta church points dataset. Figures should be treated as planning and research aids, not as final local knowledge.
