07 · Glúnta Field Guide

How to understand this town

A report begins a conversation. Combine published evidence with observation, local knowledge and careful listening.

1. Check the frame

Confirm the boundary, Census year and population covered by each measure. Write down what the report includes and which surrounding communities it leaves out.

2. Notice household life

Explore the routines of people living alone, parents, younger adults and older residents. Ask which spaces and activities are accessible across different schedules and budgets.

3. Listen across cultures

Ask residents how language, migration, faith and identity shape life here. Let people describe their own experience; a group percentage cannot explain an individual.

4. Find community assets

Record trusted people, meeting places, local services and existing partnerships. Ask what residents value and what is already working well.

5. Hear different experiences

Speak with people of different ages, backgrounds and lengths of residence. Ask permission before recording or quoting them, and distinguish one person’s account from a wider pattern.

6. Choose a small next step

Discuss what you have learned with local partners. Choose one practical way to listen, serve or collaborate, then return to ask whether it helped.

A listening and prayer walk

  1. Choose a route through housing, shops, public spaces and community facilities. Respect private property and local activity.
  2. Notice who uses each space, at what time, and who may find it difficult to access.
  3. When conversation is welcome, ask: “What do you value here?” “What has changed?” “Who should we listen to?”
  4. Pray for people and places with humility. Avoid treating observations as diagnoses of residents.
  5. Finish with three supported observations, two unanswered questions and one agreed next step.

Your local research notes

For each observation, record: place or topic · source or person’s role · date · evidence type · what remains uncertain · next action.

08 · Evidence & limits

Read the evidence carefully

  • Demographics: CSO Census 2022 Small Area Population Statistics , Built Up Areas table. Download the table used by this report . Census date: 3 April 2022. Counts are from the selected town’s published row, not a sum of intersecting Small Areas.
  • Boundary: the selected CSO urban-area feature supplied with the Glúnta map. Read about 2022 Built Up Areas . A 2022 BUA is not automatically comparable with a 2016 settlement boundary.
  • Church locations: Glúnta’s supplied church-points file . Its update date and coverage have not been established here. Listed locations are not a complete count of congregations, people or all faith communities.
  • Presentation inspiration: Brad Brisco’s sample report and Field Guide . Irish figures in this report come from the sources above.
Published statistic
A reported count with a stated date, geography and denominator.
Organisation record
A supplied listing whose coverage and currency need local verification.
Local account
A person’s experience, recorded with permission and context; not a population estimate.
Interpretation / question
A suggested meaning or research prompt, distinct from a measured fact.
Unavailable
Missing, withheld or unverified information. It must not be read as zero.

Percentages use the denominator printed on each chart. “Not stated” remains visible where the table supplies it. Rounding can cause displayed percentages to sum to 99.9% or 100.1%.

Town income, deprivation scores, health-service access and sourced local narratives are not included in this edition. Do not infer these from religion, housing tenure, birthplace or education. Add them only when their source, geography, date and method have been checked.
